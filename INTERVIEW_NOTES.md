# Interview discussion guide

## 30-second walkthrough

"I created an independent Excel FP&A model for Google Cloud, starting with seven quarters of reported segment performance. I reconciled FY2025 segment results, compared Q3 2026 performance with Q3 2025, then modeled Q4 revenue, variable and fixed cost behavior, and a possible compute-capacity premium under three cases. The dashboard links to these assumptions and extends the plan into FY2027. My main focus was separating publicly reported results from assumptions and showing the trade-off between growth and operating margins."

## Three important interview questions

**Why make up cost drivers if they are not reported?** A management-planning exercise needs cost drivers. Because no segment expense breakdown is public, I explicitly use an illustrative variable/fixed decomposition that reconciles to reported implied costs; I do not claim these are Alphabet's real expense categories.

**Why model a capacity premium?** Alphabet's October 2026 earnings commentary describes temporary pressure associated with third-party compute bridging capacity. The percentage I assign is my *scenario input*, not a company estimate.

**What would make the forecast stronger?** Internal workload utilization, TPU sales mix, contracted backlog timing, regional compute availability, sales pipeline, cost per compute-hour, headcount and server depreciation would materially sharpen the forecast. These are unavailable in public segment disclosures.

## Portfolio-integrity rule

Do not describe this independent exercise as Google employment, a delivered company forecast, a validated prediction, or evidence of achieved savings. Review each formula and be prepared to explain it before indicating that you personally built and completed the model.
